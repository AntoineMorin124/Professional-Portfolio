I'm a 4th-year Bachelor of Commerce student at the Telfer School of Management, University of Ottawa, specializing in Finance and Business Technology Management. I'm bilingual with hands-on experience in analytics, ERP systems, and project management across both the federal government and the private sector.

Right now, I work as a Junior Materiel Accountability Analyst on the Materiel Accountability team within DSCO at DND ADM(Mat), where we ensure the CAF's L1 organizations take responsibility for stocktaking their materiel and procurement. Before that, I was a Junior Financial Analyst at ADM(Mat), helping reconcile data, track finances, and prepare reports on contractors, advance payments, and inventory. I've also brought experience as a Web Analytics Officer at Employment and Social Development Canada and as a Software Tester at the Department of National Defence.

What I love most is turning complex data into something clear and useful — whether that's a Power BI dashboard, an automated workflow, or a well-structured report. I'm always looking to grow in data analytics, public sector finance, and business technology.
